Patchwork Grace @ TOA



'Patchwork Grace' - a female fronted rock band that play music that
lies somewhere between the glam and grunge catergories, with a bit of indie
garage thrown in the good luck. Each member of the band takes influenes from
differnt artists, which helps create Patchwork Grace's whismical and
inspirational sound. A few influences include Queen Adreena, Robin Black,
The Libertines and Wednesday 13


Taken By Steve Fisher

Tori Trash - Vocals
Joey Strange - Bass
Crispin Erotic - Guitar
