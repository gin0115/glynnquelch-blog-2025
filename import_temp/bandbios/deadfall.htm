Deadfall formed early 2005, bringing old school thrash metal back to where it belongs, at the forefront of metal. With influences from Megadeth and Metallica, to Dark Angel and Kreator, the sound is brutal, bone-crunching and ear battering. Now with a brand new demo out with 6 juggernaught anthems of pure metal for metalheads all over the world to listen to, Deadfall are ready to unleash their own brand of intense battering metal.

Deadfall Are:


Vocals and Guitar - Mike
Lead Guitar - Dave
Bass Guitar - Jimmi
Drums - Pete

A Deadfall live show consists of high octane head banging, crushing metal and energetic juggernaught metal right to the heart. Deadfall are described as straight old school thrash metal, the way metal should be played. Although the band are in their very early days, they have ecieved much praise for the live show and the recently cut demo. Great things await Deadfall, prepare to have your ear drums brutalised!
