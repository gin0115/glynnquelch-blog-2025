Tray Electric formed in 1980/1981, in a Nottingham womb. The next 24 years
were spent arriving here via video games, Hull, and lounging about to the
backdrop of personal and technological advances in the field of music:
oinkbeat, bassbleeps and blooplines. Jump, jump, swim, swim, punch, punch,
kicking! its way into your day. Next time you get those 8-bit blues, scan
the area for a skinnyboy with bad posture hunched over a laptop, it might be
Tray himself pushing your buttons.
