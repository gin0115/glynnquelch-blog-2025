Trippin Over Wah have been together now for a little under a year and are based in nottingham....
In such a short space of time TOW have managed to gain respect and a decent following in nottingham and proved their might with getting through to Junktion 7s BOTB semis. Even scoring them reviews and comments such as "a real class act" and "rock and roll stars in the making".

TOW's Music is often described as "contempory bluesy swagger with a touch of glam" which is absolutley bang on. It relies on kick ass riffs, power and confidence and this is definatly true if seen live!


 Trippin Over Wah Are:

Adam Kenney - Guitar/Vocals
James Medd - Guitar
Rob Milton - Bass/backing vocals
Ben Smith - Drums




Their debut single "Tokyo Joe", which was indipendantly released, has sold shed loads and with a new website under construction(www.trippin-over-wah.tk) the band are going from strength to strength!!
