'DON'T WORRY' Cerebral vacation are not going to start off by telling anyone that 'we're totally different from anything! Ever!' Because that's not true. Cerebral vacation has its own influences and originality in the way that it's been constructed - that way being thoughtfully pieced
together as apposed to following the standard a-b formula,

Catchy, melodic, heavy, intelligent, dark or moody might spring to mind? Put on the cd and read on 


Taken from www.cerebral-vacation.co.uk
 Cerebral Vacation Are

Bryn Jones - Vocals, guitar

Dom Haywood - Drums

Kosta Constantinou - Guitar

Lance Leivers - Vocals, bass

The band is coming down hard on the local live circuit with their energetic, full on performances that always contains a good mixture of new and old material.

"Tightmelodicentertainingthey rocked, nice riffs, great stage presence" Gigged

They have performed loads of gigs in Nottingham including venues like the Old Angel, junction 7 and Mansfield's Town Mill and many more around the UK + two trips to Germany in 2004 + 2005 playing in underground metal venues and clubs.

"This is obviously a band that knows far more than just their onions" Plusone Magazine

Cerebral Vacation had been a three piece for about nine months (December 2003 - august 2004) and had always seemed confident and satisfied with their sound and performances however, Lance (bassist) put the idea of a second guitarist to Bryn and Dom and they were both up for the idea, So in late august (2004) Kosta Constantinou (long time school friend) was added to the band as a second guitarist alternating rhythm and lead with Bryn. After a few practises with him and a brief home recording session and the whole band are confident that he is only going to make the band stronger.

"This is beautiful metal, with passionate, frequently brutal and enigmatic complexity." Saggy Pants
