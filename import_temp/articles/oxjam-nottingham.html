Oxjam is Oxfam's biggest, most-ambitious, music event ever. This October Oxfam is asking everyone to get involved and raise money for charity, be it small venues, big arenas, huge millionaire bands or local unsigned acts so of course this October Nottingham's music scene will be a lively place!

There are loads of ways you can support Oxjam:

- You could organise an oxjam night with other bands you know.

- Or if you've got gigs in October anyway - you could promote the gig as part of Oxjam and donate proceeds towards Oxfam's work.

- You could ask your venue to make a donation from the cloakroom and/or admission charge or from a collection at the bar.

The possibilities are endless, and it is up to you to make it happen.

For more information contact:

Loads of people are already getting involved including BBC Nottingham, Chris McDonald/Filthy Rat Promotions, Demo student night, our friends at Drop In The Ocean, Moshmonkey and I Dress Myself and of course Saggy Pants will also be helping out!

On Sunday 29th of October the Hockley Hustle is a big event at bars in Hockley including The Old Angel, The Social, Bunkers Hill and Broadway and involves bands such as The Silver Tongues, Old Basford, Yes my Ninjas, Clarky Cat, The Wickets, Andy Whittle, Back Goldsmith and Rimtumbler.
