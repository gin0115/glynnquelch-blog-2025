A problem that has plagued the Nottingham scene for a while now is that people don't know where and when to go see the local bands. Alot of Venues do little to no promotion work, and there aren't enough Promoters to go around. So we thought for our second issue we would provide a brief guide to Nottingham's smaller live music venues and promoters, because there is a real buzz about Nottingham's scene, you just got to listen hard enough.
