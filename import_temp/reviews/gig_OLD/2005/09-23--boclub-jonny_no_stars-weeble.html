Breakneck + Jimmy The Squirrel + Jonny No Stars + Weeble

23/09/05 - The Boat Club(Nottingham)

Brixton's music scene was at its finest and on show at this ska/punk fest. London boys Breakneck graced the stage. Stripped to the bear bone from there normal electric line up, they entertained us with a 3 piece acoustic set and they didn't disappoint. Strong guitar was complimented with an effective bass style, fit to grace any stage and far from his normal style, the singer showed his amazing vocal range with a Brandon Boyd style performance that left the crown in awe.

Next on stage also keeping Brixton music alive in Nottingham, the Skank-tastic, Jimmy The Squirrel. Visualize Desmond Decker and Bob Marley donating sperm to the same woman to create a child that would be Jimmy The Squirrel all in one (lucky little chap). Bassline pumping to the sweet Ska sound, being beautifully crafted by the guitarist and an Hamond organ sound and trombone, contributing to a feel good, laid back sound and strong vocal harmony that make them great.

Jonny No Stars gave us a slightly different style of punk, more aggressive with a rock edge. Monster guitar riffs with some ska in places, their effective presence and stong proformance added to a fantastic un-missable night.

Buzzing with excitement from a fantasic line up, the crowd were in for an unmissable treat , gracing the stage were 8 piece ska-punkers, Weeble. From the start they had us all dancing, smiling and singing along, even though some of the crowd didn't know the words. You could not escape the vibe that is Weeble! The audience participation in the last song, 'Hooked Up', left the crowd begging for more.



Review - Pedro (Fly On The Wall Promotions)
