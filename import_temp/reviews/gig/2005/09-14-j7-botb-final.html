Junktion 7`s Battle Of The Bands Final
The Starrlings + Balance Of Silence + Deadfall + Spirytus

14/09/04 - Junktion 7 (Nottingham)

There's no mistake, I smell that smell, it's that time of year again. The Junktion 7 Battle of the Bands 2005 is at an end and the fate of the four remaining bands is to be decided.
So we're battered (again)
The notepad has been complacently dismissed and we're going commando. Armed with nothing but a beer and bag of amazing grace, we are attracted to Junktion 7 like flies round a dung feast fit for shit-headed Kings of Crap.
Over the last 10 weeks we have seen many weird and wonderful things and the prospect of tonight's finale, has us in a frenzied and upbeat mood (or that could be the Kronenberg).
So who will be claimed winners of this years Junktion 7 BOTB???
Will it be the rock n roll swagger of The Starrlings? Mixing their catchy, upbeat and moving melodies with energy and charisma, which has proven to win over even the most metal hardened crowds?
Will it be the sheer power of Leicester 's finest hardcore act Balance Of Silence? Who impress with stupendous interlinking guitar solos and a frontman who not only switches his vocals between belting screams and emotional melodies, but has the friendliest of veneers between songs, putting the crowd at ease.
Will it be the full-blown old skool thrash of Deadfall? With their super fast solos, dynamic and imaginative drumming and pure power in their angry vocals and booming basslines.
Will it be the energetic and emotion fuelled metal of Spirytus? Whose frontman has more get-up-and-go than the Duracell rabbit and with great instrumental riffs that build and move, finally morphing into songs that full of feeling.

The Starrlings were up first and pulling out all the stops they put on the sort of performance we have become used to. They appeal to a wide range of people and the whole crowd seemed to get into it. It is everything you want to kick the night off to a great start.
Next Balance of Silence crashed onto the stage putting in a great show. It was fierce and professional, showing some awesome technical ability. For such a heavy band it has a good range of emotion and still has some very catchy melodies.
Third to the stage were Deadfall, whose fast and furious guitar riffs and deep vocals mixed with the tightest of drumming, really imprints a powerful image in your brain. They also seem to have a devoted following and the crowd really get into it, head banging as far as the eye can see!
Finally the last band to lay their claim to the title of Junktion 7 2005 BOTB champs was Spirytus. Using some cool lighting, smoke and intro music to make sure everyone knew they'd arrived, they looked really up for it. The frontman, who has been lively in ever show we have seen, really goes mental, running around the room and getting the crowd really fired up. The music moves beautifully to make it a really hard decision.

While the judges made their minds up we were treated to one final act and that was last years winner Weeble. We skanked our arses off for15 minutes to their punk-ska madness, before Tami appeared to make the announcement.
First were the individual prizes.
Best Guitarist goes to Birdbrain.
Best Drummer goes to Deadfall.
Best Bassist goes to Conceptual Misfire.
Best Vocalist goes to Spirytus. (wooahh I can't believe we remembered all that!)
Next up the Runners up in this year Junktion 7 Battle of the Bands wereSpirytus.

After 11 weeks of drunken dancing, stoned skanking and witnessing such wonders as Air Biscuit's musical acid trip, Cerebral Vacations power metal mayhem, Jimmy the Squirrel's un-stoppable ska machine, the kids showing us how its done with young bands such as Answers Within and Trippin Over Wah kicking out the jams and much more the moment of truth is upon us.

Tonight's final, where all 4 bands really showed us that Nottingham has some awesome talent, here it is the winners of Junktion 7 Battle of the Bands 2005 were.Deadfall!!!!!!

So well done them! Thanks for entertaining us and for all of you who missed it, NAR NAR NAR NAR NAR!
