Gauntleys Of NottinghamPrivate Blend No2

Blended By: Glynn Quelch

Manufactured By: GQ Tobaccos

Contents: Burley, Virginia, Black Cavendish

Cut: Broken Flake

Country: United Kingdom

Production: No longer in production

Description

The Gauntleys Private Blends are custom tobaccos created in the Nottingham shop for specific customers. A selection of these will be released for general sale.

Notes

Notes: Private Blend No2 is an Aromatic, but nothing like the American or European blends out there. Built on a heavy foundation of Sliced Brown twist soaked in Rum with added Black & Gold Cavendish cased with Spices & Honey. This blend is smooth & cool to smoke but carries a big hit of Nicotine. Originally a blend smoked only by Glynn. First created in early 2011.
