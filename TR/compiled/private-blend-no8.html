Description

The Gauntleys Private Blends are custom tobaccos created in the Nottingham shop for specific customers. A selection of these will be released for general sale.

Notes

Notes: Private Blend No8 was first created for an internet customer who liked the sour and smoky tastes of Balkan/Oriental blends but found many of them to rich & powerful. Based on our Yataghan tobacco, with extra Sun/Flue cured Virginias and a small pinch of Latakia. With this being a light blend, dont expect a big bold smoke, although this does make a great introduction to the smoky classics on offer.
