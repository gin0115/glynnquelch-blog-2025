Description

A punchy blend of black Virginias, spicy perique, and deep earthy Kentucky. Black Lace uses a large (20%) helping of perique: at this level the perique is sour and very spicy, so a fragrant base of black Virginia is added to create a sweet and spicy flavour. And then, some earthy Kentucky is added to give some body, like a good Stout or Porter. If you're looking for a grassy VaPer, then look elsewhere!
