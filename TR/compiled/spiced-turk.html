Description

A subtly sweet but mainly savory Dutch style “Dry” aromatic, not depending heavily on lots of casing to create a complex flavour. The blend is made up a (non goopy) Black Cavendish, Virginias & Turkish. With the added flavours of sweet rose, cinnamon & nutmeg. The use of Turkish tobacco & sweet rose, which tastes a little like Turkish Delight.
