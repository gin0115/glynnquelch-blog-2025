Description

What began as a request for menthol pipe tobacco turned out to be on the difficult side for Glynn to make. Not being the biggest fan of menthol the sampling was not the highlight of the process. Made from a classic American style aromatic with black cavendish and mixed Virginias. The creamy and cool base is topped of with fresh cherries and a cool and crisp menthol.
