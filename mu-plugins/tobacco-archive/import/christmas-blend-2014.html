Description

Every year we release our annual Christmas blend and for 2014 we have kept with a similar blend as last year. Think of the traditional aroma of Christmas and that's what we have here. Plenty of spices; cinnamon, all spice, nutmeg, clove, star anise and of course it has to have some booze in there. We also opted for a generous helping of both orange and brandy.
