Description

Every tobacco manufacturer has one - call it Pensioners Mix, El Cheapo, Frankenstein Mixture- they are all the same. The leftovers from packaging batches, blends which have been incorrectly mixed, and our "Blenders Special" is no different. This tobacco is a little bit of everything, sometimes when we weigh out 10 gram samples we end up with grams left over. These are added to the tobacco and mixed in. You can have literally a different taste with every bowl and makes for an interesting all day smoke.
