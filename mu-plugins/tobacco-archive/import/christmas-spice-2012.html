Description

Due to the lack of Kretek cigar leaf, we have had to change our popular Christmas blend from this year. While still being based on the same concept the 2012 blend still carries the core of mulled mixed fruits, traditional Christmas spices and a large dash of Dark Rum.

Notes

Notes: Made from a mixture of Virginia, Black & Gold Cavendish's, Twist and a small helping of Burley. Flavoured with mixed mulled fruits (cherry, plum, berries), Vanilla, Cinnamon, Nutmeg, Ginger, Cognac & Rum.
